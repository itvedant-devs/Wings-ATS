    * **Grammar & Spelling:** Rigorously check for errors.

    * **Message:** "No specific Job Description (JD) was provided. Performing a General ATS Optimization Review to enhance resume compatibility with various ATS systems and general recruiter best practices."

        * **Example:** "Effective use of strong action verbs at the start of most bullet points (e.g., 'Developed', 'Managed', 'Implemented')."
    * **General Areas for Improvement (for Universal ATS Compatibility):**
        * Provide **2-3 concise bullet points** on areas that could be improved for better general ATS performance.
        * **For each suggestion, provide a concrete example of how the line/word could be rephrased or what specific detail could be added, offering a fully rewritten line where applicable.**
